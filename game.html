The Missing Trophy

About the Game

The school trophy is missing and there is a $1,000 reward for finding it.

The Story

You saw the trophy yesterday, but now it is gone. You decide to look around the school and find it.

How to Play

Follow the clues and make choices to find the trophy. If you find it, you get the $1,000 reward.
